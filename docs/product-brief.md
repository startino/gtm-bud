## **Core Value Proposition**

An AI‑driven outreach generator that gives solo consultants and small service providers a complete go‑to‑market plan and personalized outreach in hours - no SDR team, no complex setup, no agency retainers.

---

## **Primary Job to Be Done**

Help individuals who are starting their business create and execute personalized outreach campaigns that generate initial revenue and pipeline without hiring an agency.

---

## **Key User Persona Details**

- **Profile:** Solo consultant, newly independent professional, or recently laid‑off expert with limited sales/tech confidence.
- **Pain Points:** Doesn’t know how to build consistent pipeline. Limited budget to hire agencies (~$500/month max)…
- **Buying Triggers:** Actively trying to start a business or validate a service idea.
- **Objections:** Skeptical that AI can produce relevant, high‑quality leads/messages (and strategies?)
- **Success Metric:** Gets first paying client within 45 days of use.

---

## **MVP Success Criteria (KPIs)**

- 10 Evangelists (users who love the product so much they actively recommend it to others  **because they have gotten results**)
- Result = 1 client closed from outbound campaign

---

## **Product Scope Limits (MVP)**

- Single‑user workflow only (no team or CRM sync).
- Manual campaign execution - user sends outreach manually.
- CSV export only (no email or LinkedIn automation yet).

---

## **How It Works**

1. **User Inputs:**
    
    The user provides a few key details
    
    - Their LinkedIn profile
    - Their website
2. **AI Generation:**
    
    The system uses ****AI to analyze this data and create:
    
    - “Value Offer” outreach strategies
    - User chooses their favorite strategy
    - User enters a sample profile URL(s)
    - Shows ICP attributes
    - User refines ICP
    - Example messages are generated for that sample profile URL
    - User chooses their favorite message
    - User receives a sample lead list (5-10)
    - Outreach messages tailored to real prospect profiles.
3. **Output / Deliverable (emailed within 24hrs):**
    
    The product would output a **spreadsheet (CSV)** of:
    
    - Highly targeted leads (found through tools like **Exa** and **Clay**)
    - Corresponding personalized messages ready to send.
    
    Optionally, it could also flag leads with **open LinkedIn profiles**, so users can contact them instantly if they have a **Sales Navigator** account
    
4. **User Experience:**
    
    Users can review the suggested copy and targeting (maybe start with a sample), then request generation of 100-500 more leads and messages like those.
    
5. Campaign Management:
    
    Our tool will only generate the initial assets so that the user can carry out the campaigns themselves.

## **Business Model**

**Strategic Fit with Agency Brand**

The SaaS acts as a **low‑ticket feeder product** for Referral Program Pros.

- Converts a segment that’s too small for managed services.
- Builds a warm pipeline of users that can later upgrade to full agency retainers.

**Revenue Model**

Likely **low-to-mid-ticket**, priced **per lead or batch** ($0.5 per lead), avg. LTV per user $X
